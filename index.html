Forest

A forest is a large area covered with trees and undergrowth. Forests are home to a vast variety of plants and animals.
They play an essential role in maintaining the Earth’s ecological balance by producing oxygen, conserving water, and providing shelter to wildlife.
Forests are divided into different types like tropical, temperate, and boreal, each supporting different species based on climate and geography.
